Controlled threshold + primary strength

Only hard lower-body day and two-a-day. Keep threshold submaximal after hard Nov 14 long run. No lower-body AMRAPs.

DurationRun ~55–65 min; lifting ~50–60 min, separated by several hours
Volume3 × 6 min RPE 6–7 with 2 min jog + controlled primary strength
Footwear / surfaceSupportive running trainers on flat measured road/track; stable gym shoes for lifting

Coaching note: Only genuine lower-body high day and true two-a-day. Due to Nov 14's hard long run and absent lift/tissue detail, do not increase to 4 reps or race threshold pace. Green required for any intensity.

Decision gate and warm-up

  • Green / amber / red check: GREEN: normal gait, typical easy HR/effort, no focal discomfort, prior long-run soreness resolving. AMBER: stale/heavy legs, low readiness or persistent soreness; use shorter option. RED: painful/altered gait, illness or unusual fatigue; no threshold or lower lifting.
  • Easy warm-up: 12–15 min easy RPE 2–3; ankle rocker 8/side, walking lunge 5/side, leg swing 8/side.
  • Relaxed preparation strides: 2–3 × 15–20 sec smooth progressive, NOT sprinting; 60–75 sec easy jog. Skip if lower leg unhappy.

Threshold — primary aerobic stimulus

  • 3 × 6 min controlled threshold: RPE 6–7 (comfortably hard) with 2:00 easy jog after reps 1–2. Rep 1 intentionally conservative; avoid using Nov 10 faster splits as a pace target.
  • Record each rep: Log rep pace, average HR and RPE, plus whether breathing/stride remained controlled; should be able to do another rep.
  • Cooldown: 10–12 min easy; end early if mechanics deteriorate. Stop if rep 1 >RPE 7.5, pain appears or normal form fails.

Primary strength — hours later

  • Back squat: 2 × 4 at RPE 7–7.5; 3 min rest; no forced reps or load progression.
  • Bench press: 3 × 5 at RPE 7–7.5; 2.5–3 min rest.
  • Rear-foot-elevated split squat: 1–2 × 5 each leg at RPE 6–7; 90–120 sec; omit for lingering fatigue.
  • Chest-supported row: 3 × 8–10 at RPE 7; 90 sec.
  • Standing calf raise: 2 × 8–10 at RPE 7, controlled eccentric; 75–90 sec. Omit if Achilles irritable.

Fuel and readiness alternatives

  • High-day fueling: Carbohydrate and fluid before running; substantial carb/protein meal between sessions; 25–40 g protein + carbohydrate after lifting. Avoid a large energy shortfall.
  • AMBER: 2 × 6 min threshold at RPE ≤6–6.5, 2 min jog; upper-only bench/row in PM, no lower body; or easy aerobic if recovery not good.
  • RED: 25–40 min very easy bike only if symptom-free; otherwise rest. Skip threshold and lower-body lifting. Seek assessment for worsening/localized symptoms.

Conversational aerobic development. Four short smooth uphill strides only for fully green legs; finish by walking, without added jogging cooldown.

Duration65–70 min running; strides/walk-back may extend clock time slightly
Volume65–70 min easy RPE 2–3, then 0–4 × 8 sec controlled uphill strides
Footwear / surfaceSupportive trainers on flat/gentle running route; dry predictable 3–5% uphill only for optional strides

Coaching note: Aerobic development first; hill strides provide a little acceleration stimulus, not max velocity. Complete all jogging before the strides; walk home/cool down without extra jogging. Suppress hills if Tuesday still in the legs.

Readiness gate

  • Tissue and fatigue screen: Only do the full 65–70 min if Tuesday is absorbed, gait normal and no focal calf/Achilles, hamstring, foot or adductor discomfort. Sunday 3/5 readiness and Saturday intensity require restraint.
  • Start easy: First 10–15 min especially gentle; avoid steep descents, racing companions or an unplanned progression.

Easy aerobic volume

  • 65–70 min easy run: RPE 2–3 conversational, flat-to-gently rolling, no steady section. Hold lower-than-usual pace if needed after Tuesday.
  • No extra running after strides: Finish the planned easy running before hill strides; walking recovery and a short walk home are sufficient.

Optional strides — GREEN tissue only

  • 4 × 8 sec moderate uphill: Smooth build to ~85–90% effort on dry 3–5% incline. Walk back; at least 2–3 min between; no bounding, timing or racing.
  • Skip criteria: Omit all strides if tired calves/Achilles, hamstring or adductor, localized discomfort, heavy legs or asymmetrical push-off. No running cooldown.
  • Stop rule: End the set immediately for sharp sensation, shortened/asymmetric stride or poor coordination.

Fuel and alternatives

  • Fuel: Normal carbohydrate-containing meal/snack before, fluid as needed, carb/protein meal after. 65–70 min easy generally does not require aggressive midrun fueling.
  • AMBER: 45–55 min easy with no strides; if impact-sensitive, 45–55 min easy bike instead.
  • RED: Rest or very easy non-impact only if comfortable; no hills or running through altered gait.

Easy long run (no surges)

75–80 min deliberately easy; prior Saturday was fast and should not be repeated as a disguised workout. No progression or fast finish.

Duration75–80 min
Volume75–80 min conversational easy; no tempo, hills surges, or fast finish
Footwear / surfaceSupportive trainers on flat/gentle predictable route

Coaching note: Nov 14's 10.60 miles in 77:16 (HR147 avg, one 6:01 mile) was not the easy long-run stimulus intended. Correct intensity this week rather than extending duration or chasing distance.

Start and long-run execution

  • Readiness check: Only run 75–80 min with normal gait, recovered lower legs and routine fatigue. Use 60–70 min when amber.
  • Easy long run: 75–80 min at RPE 2–3, full sentences, first 15 min especially slow. Keep effort near personal genuinely easy range rather than pace from Nov 14.
  • No fast finish: Do not include threshold repeats, 6-minute miles, pickups or hill sprints; if effort climbs, slow down or shorten.
  • Stop rule: Stop for focal worsening pain, obvious gait change, dizziness or unusual exhaustion; substitute rest/non-impact only if comfortable.

Fuel

  • Before: 30–60 g carbohydrate 1–2 hr beforehand as tolerated; adjust meal timing to gut comfort.
  • During: Water/electrolytes according to weather and sweating; ~30–45 g carbohydrate/hr optional-to-useful for 75–80 min, especially if otherwise underfueled.
  • After: Within a normal meal, 25–40 g protein plus substantial carbohydrates; replace fluids/sodium as needed.

Alternatives and logging

  • AMBER: 60–70 min easy, or 70–80 min easy cycle if impact soreness; no make-up mileage.
  • RED: Rest for pain/altered gait or illness.
  • Record: Log actual duration, distance, average HR, pace and RPE; tissue and next-morning response. This is not a time trial.
