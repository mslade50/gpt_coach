Upper A + easy run

Single training window; Upper A first, then relaxed aerobic run. Leave legs fresh after recent 51.4-mile week.

Duration~85–105 min total
Workload14 upper working sets + 40–45 min easy run
Footwear / surfaceStable gym shoes then comfortable neutral trainers; level or gently rolling ground

Coach's note: Upper A comes first. This is not a second high day. Progression and AMRAPs inappropriate after a 51.4-mi week.

Before and Upper A

  • Readiness gate: Start only with normal gait and no concerning focal pain. Sleep/fatigue below usual: reduce to 2 sets per large lift and 25–30 min easy run. No added leg work.
  • Incline dumbbell press: 3 × 8–10 @ RPE 7–7.5; 90 sec rest; last rep should not grind.
  • Neutral-grip pull-up or pulldown: 3 × 8–10 @ RPE 7–8; 90 sec rest.
  • Chest-supported row: 2 × 10–12 @ RPE 7; 75–90 sec rest.
  • DB lateral raise: 2 × 15–20 @ RPE 7; 45–60 sec rest.
  • Curl: 2 × 10–15 with 2–3 reps in reserve; 60 sec rest.
  • Cable pressdown: 2 × 10–15 with 2–3 reps in reserve; 60 sec rest.

Easy aerobic immediately after

  • Run: 40–45 min @ RPE 2–3; conversational on flat/gentle road or path; first 10 min deliberately slow. No strides, hills or faster last mile.
  • No extra workout: Finish after the run; do not add another evening session or compensate for reduced weekly mileage.

Fuel and modifications

  • Fuel: Eat carbohydrate-containing meal/snack before this combined session; afterward 25–40 g protein and a substantial carbohydrate serving. Replace fluids and sodium as needed.
  • AMBER: Upper A: 2 × per major lift, 1 × accessories; then 25–30 min easy run or easy bike.
  • RED: Rest if pain changes gait, illness or profound fatigue.
High Tue Nov 24, 2026 · Readiness-gated

Speed retention + controlled primary strength

Only high lower-body day and only two-a-day. Default controlled accelerations; full fast flying exposures only with verified recent sprint tolerance and quiet tissues.

DurationAM ~35–50 min; PM ~45–60 min separated by 4–6+ hours
WorkloadDefault 2 × 20–30 m controlled acceleration; GREEN & verified fast tolerance 3 × 20–30 m accel + 2 × fly 20 m; PM primary lift
Footwear / surfaceDry level track or predictable straightaway in cushioned trainers; stable gym footwear for lifting

Coach's note: Latest Training Log documents fast 1-mi repetitions, not a verified near-maximal sprint session. Therefore default is submaximal controlled accelerations, NOT automatic flying 20s. Do not assume green tissues.

AM readiness and warm-up

  • GREEN gate: Full speed only with 0–1/10 localized calf/Achilles, hamstring, adductor and foot issues, normal gait, springy warm-up, AND documented near-max running tolerated recently. If the latter is unknown, use default accelerations.
  • Easy jog: 12–15 min very easy.
  • Movement prep: Ankle rockers 8/side; adductor rockbacks 6/side; leg swings 8/side; walking lunge 5/side; A-march 20 m and A-skip 20 m.
  • Buildups: 2 × 40–50 m smooth at ~60% then ~75–80%; 2–3 min walk.

AM speed choices — not additive

  • DEFAULT: 2 × 20–30 m smooth accelerations: ~85–90% effort on level dry surface, 4–5 min walk between; no timing; stop if sluggish or forced. No flying reps.
  • GREEN + known recent fast-running tolerance ONLY: 3 × 20–30 m progressive acceleration @ ~90–93%, full 4–5 min rest; THEN 2 × flying 20 m using 25–30 m build-up @ fast but relaxed ~90–93% with 5–6 min recovery. No racing, no spikes.
  • Cool down: 8–12 min easy jog/walk if desired, contained in total AM time; skip if any tissue signal. Log exact reps and sensations.

PM primary lift, several hours later

  • Readiness recheck: Full lower-body lift only with normal post-sprint gait and no tissue signal. Recent lower lifting unverified; begin conservatively.
  • Back squat or trap-bar deadlift — choose one: 2 × 4 squat OR 2 × 3 trap-bar deadlift @ RPE 6.5–7; 3 min rest; no maximal pull and no grinding.
  • Bench press: 3 × 5 @ RPE 7–7.5; 2½–3 min rest.
  • Rear-foot-elevated split squat: 1 × 5 each leg @ RPE ≤6.5; 90–120 sec rest; omit if heavy legs.
  • Chest-supported row: 3 × 8 @ RPE 7; 90 sec rest.
  • Standing calf raise: 2 × 8–10 @ RPE 6–7; 75–90 sec; only pain-free.

Fuel, AMBER/RED and stop

  • Fuel: Carbohydrate before AM; full carbohydrate/protein meal between sessions; 25–40 g protein plus carbohydrate after PM. Hydrate across the day; no intentional underfueling.
  • AMBER: Easy 30–40 min aerobic instead of sprinting if fatigued; PM upper-only bench/rows if appropriate, no lower-body strength. If symptoms from speed appear, stop entirely.
  • RED / stop: Stop speed immediately for sharp/localized pain, altered gait, loss of spring, coordination deterioration, or repeated 3–4% performance drop. Rest and arrange evaluation for persistent or worsening symptoms.

Short controlled subthreshold

A-week aerobic quality without second high day. Recovered legs only, no lifting. Do not replicate Nov 17's faster mile intervals.

Duration~50–55 min
Workload2 × 8 min RPE 5–6 / 2 min jog; rest easy
Footwear / surfaceDaily trainers on level measured path or track; avoid steep/technical terrain

Coach's note: Goal: controlled 10K/aerobic development without another hard day. Nov 17's aggressive fast-mile session does not justify progressing Thursday. Use talk test and RPE, not 5:51 mile pace.

Before and warm-up

  • Readiness gate: Only do subthreshold if Tuesday's speed/lifting was absorbed, gait normal and no focal foot/calf/Achilles/hamstring/adductor symptoms. Heavy legs = easy instead.
  • Warm-up: 15 min very easy jog then 2–3 min light dynamic mobility; no sprint strides needed.

Main aerobic block

  • 2 × 8 min subthreshold: RPE 5–6, comfortably strong but clearly below threshold; 2 min light jog between. Start conservative, stable form/breathing, no forcing a pace.

  • Stop rule: If HR/effort drifts sharply, form changes, RPE rises above 6, or local pain develops, turn the session into easy running and stop intensity.

Fuel and modifications

  • Fuel: Carbohydrate-containing snack/meal before; normal carb/protein meal afterward; water as needed.
  • AMBER: One × 8 min @ RPE ≤5, or 40–45 min fully easy; no doubles or lower-body strength.
  • RED: 20–40 min comfortable non-impact only if symptom-free; otherwise rest.

Aerobic volume Sat Nov 28, 2026 · Readiness-gated

Long easy run without pace chasing

Return long-run intensity to truly easy instead of Nov 21's 7:25 average. No 6–7 min fast miles, surges or progression.

Duration75–80 min
Workload75–80 min RPE 2–3 only; 60–65 min amber
Footwear / surfaceComfortable trainers on flat or gently rolling, safe predictable route

Coach's note: Nov 21 11.15 mi in 82:42 at 7:25/mi HR147 was too brisk to assume easy. This week pace must be slower than fast recent long-run miles as needed to preserve genuinely easy effort. Do not increase volume.

Before/during run

  • Readiness gate: Green if normal gait, no progressing localized pain and routine fatigue. Recent 51.43-mi week and strength/speed on Tuesday make restraint essential.
  • Warm into easy pace: First 15 min especially relaxed, RPE 2; then 60–65 min conversational RPE 2–3, no target pace.
  • No fast segments: No pickups, hills sprints, threshold, surges or fast finish. Reduce pace for hills; if HR/RPE climbs without reason, shorten.
  • Stop: Stop for focal sharp or worsening pain, gait change, dizziness or unusual exertion. Rest instead of making up minutes.

Fuel/hydration

  • Before: ~30–60 g carbohydrate 1–2 hr before as tolerated; consider larger pre-run meal if chronically underfueled.
  • During: Water and sodium matched to conditions and sweat loss; 30–45 g carbohydrate per hour is reasonable for this duration if pre-run fueling is light.
  • After: Normal meal with 25–40 g protein, substantial carbohydrate, fluids and salt as needed.

Alternatives and record

  • AMBER: 60–65 min easy, or 60–75 min low-impact bike if impact-sensitive; no additional running later.
  • RED: Rest if meaningful focal pain or illness.
  • Log: Actual minutes, distance, average HR, RPE, any faster miles and next-morning tissue score. Aim to avoid repeating HR147/7:25 as a purported easy day.
