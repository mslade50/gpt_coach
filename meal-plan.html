SEVEN-DAY ROTATION

Same base. Dinner variety.

Use the daily meals below each day. Every dinner starts with 160 g cooked protein, 80 g dry rice, and 200 g vegetables. Choose fats to fit the dinner estimate; salmon replaces chicken.

Monday

Chicken & rice

Chicken, rice, and vegetables. Finish with a measured portion of feta or olive oil.

Snack rotation: walnuts
Tuesday

Salmon rice bowl

Salmon, rice, and vegetables. Recalibrate added fats for the salmon swap.

Snack rotation: almonds
Wednesday

Chicken & vegetables

Chicken and rice with a different vegetable mix. Use a measured olive-oil serving.

Snack rotation: pistachios
Thursday

Chicken & feta bowl

Chicken, rice, and vegetables with calibrated feta. Count feta toward the dinner fats.

Snack rotation: walnuts
Friday

Chicken rice plate

Chicken, rice, and vegetables. An egg can replace part of the added-fat allowance.

Snack rotation: almonds
Saturday

Salmon & vegetables

Salmon with rice and vegetables. Adjust oil or cheese rather than adding all fats together.

Snack rotation: pistachios
Sunday

Simple chicken bowl

Chicken, rice, and vegetables. Use the remaining calibrated fats and prep the next batch.

Snack rotation: walnuts

The rotation is an example built from the template options. Nut swaps use equal calories, not automatically equal gram weights. Add the sports-fuel swap only on a day when you actually use it.

THE DAILY BASE

Meals & portions

Keep the base simple, then log the portions and labels you actually use.

Pre-run

410 kcal

2 Welch’s pouches + 3 Rice Krispies Treats.

4 g protein · 0 g fiber

Regular bars and pouches assumed; check labels. This block is included in the daily estimate.

Breakfast

945 kcal
  • 80 g oats + 200 g nonfat yogurt + 150 g berries
  • 250 ml skim milk + 60 g Cinnamon Toast Crunch
  • 240 ml orange juice
50 g protein · 16 g fiber

After the morning run; the day’s one yogurt meal.

Lunch

815 kcal
  • 125 g cooked chicken
  • 3 wheat bread slices, each about 110 kcal and 4 g fiber
  • 40 g peanut butter + 15 g honey
60 g protein · 16 g fiber

The template does not require extra vegetables or oil at lunch.

Afternoon snack

530 kcal

A 300 kcal serving of pretzels + approximately 35 g walnuts, or another nut serving calibrated to the same calories.

12 g protein · 5 g fiber

On big days, replace some pretzels with sports fuel using the swap below.

Dinner

890 kcal
  • 160 g cooked chicken or salmon
  • 80 g dry rice + 200 g vegetables
  • Measured fats or cheese chosen to meet the dinner calories
60 g protein · 7 g fiber

This is the template estimate. Calibrate oil, feta, eggs, or salmon; avoid double-counting fats.

Dessert smoothie

405 kcal
  • 250 ml skim milk + 1 whey scoop
  • 30 g ice cream
  • 150 g mango + 150 g strawberries
35 g protein · 6 g fiber

No second yogurt serving needed.

BIG-DAY SWAP

Sports fuel counts toward the day

An 80 kcal sports-powder serving + a gel with 50 g carbohydrate (about 200 kcal) totals about 280 kcal. Replace about 280 kcal of pretzels to keep the example day near its original total. Log the actual products and amounts.

ONE WEEK AT A TIME

Shopping & prep

Quantities below cover seven days of the example base and the five-chicken / two-salmon dinner rotation. Adjust for meals you replace.
