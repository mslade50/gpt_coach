GREEN/AMBER gate and warm-up

  • Gate: GREEN only if normal gait, adequate energy, hamstring/calf/Achilles/foot/adductor symptoms 0–1/10, mechanics unguarded and recent fast-running tolerance known. Otherwise AMBER.
  • Jog and mobility: 12–15 min easy jogging; ankle rockers 8/side, leg swings 8/side, walking lunges 5/side.
  • Sprint drills: A-march 20m; A-skip 20m; low dribble 20m. Low pogos 2×6 only fully green.
  • Buildups: 3–4×40–50m progressing ~60/75/85/(90)% with full walkback/rest.

Track speed — GREEN only

  • Acceleration: 3×20–30m two-point starts at ~92–95% current max, 4–5 min full recovery; relaxed quality.
  • Flying 20s: 2×flying 20m: 30–35m progressive buildup, 20m fly, 30–40m gradual deceleration at ~93–95% current feel; 5–7 min full rest.
  • Speed stop rule: Stop for any focal pain, asymmetry, protective gait, sudden slowdown or loss of smoothness. No timed testing or extra running quota.

AMBER / unverified fast-running history

  • Controlled starts only: 2×20m at ~85–90% with 4–5 min full rest; NO flying sprints or maximal effort. If awkward or sore, drop accelerations and finish easy or stop.

Primary lift, several hours later

  • Deadlift: Warm up in small steps; 2×3 @ RPE 6.5–7 with 3–4 min rest. Do not assume old maximal loads still apply.
  • Bench press: 3×5 @ RPE 7–7.5; 2.5–3 min rest, account for Monday pressing.
  • Rear-foot-elevated split squat: 1–2×5 each leg @ RPE 6.5–7; 90–120 sec. Omit for meaningful leg fatigue.
  • Chest-supported row: 2×8–10 @ RPE 7–8; 90 sec rest.
  • Calf raises, optional: 1–2×8–10 @ RPE 7; 75–90 sec only with absolutely quiet Achilles/calf.

Between sessions, alternatives

  • Fuel: High-carbohydrate food plus 25–40g protein after track; another substantial carbohydrate/protein meal between sessions; replenish fluids/sodium.
  • AMBER strength: Bench + row as tolerated; skip deadlift/RFESS/calf loading for lower-body symptoms. Do not turn into a second hard leg session.
  • RED: No sprinting or loaded legs for sharp/worsening/gait-altering pain, illness, or pronounced systemic fatigue; rest or comfortable non-impact only if appropriate.
