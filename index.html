NON-NEGOTIABLE

Quality governs volume

01

Sprint performance

End or modify acceleration work when times deteriorate about 3–4%, projection meaningfully worsens, or two consecutive reps lose quality.

02

Plyometric contacts

Stop or regress when output drops about 5%, contacts become slow or loud, landing position deteriorates, or asymmetry appears.

03

Tissue response

Sharp, localized, worsening or gait-altering pain is not normal fatigue. Do not sprint or perform intensive jumps through it.
