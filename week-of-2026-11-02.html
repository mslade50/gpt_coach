Readiness and Upper A — lift first

  • Incline barbell press OR incline DB press: 3 × 8 at RPE 7–8, ~2 reps in reserve; 90–120 sec rest. Reduce from Oct 26's 145 lb if necessary to avoid RPE 9.

  • Chest-supported row: 2 × 10–12 at RPE 7–8; 75–90 sec rest.
  • Lateral raises: 2 × 15–20 at RPE 7–8; 45–60 sec rest.
  • Curl: 2 × 10–15, 1–2 reps in reserve; 60 sec rest.
  • Pressdown: 2 × 10–15, 1–2 reps in reserve; 60 sec rest.

Easy aerobic — immediately afterward

Fueling and alternatives

  • Fueling: Carbohydrate-containing snack/meal beforehand; 25–40 g protein and carbohydrate afterward; fluids to thirst and conditions.
  • Amber: 2 upper sets per exercise + 30–35 min easy run or bike. If impact tenderness, bike instead.
  • Red / stop: Stop running for sharp, worsening, asymmetric, or gait-altering pain; skip the whole session for illness or serious systemic fatigue.

Controlled threshold + primary strength

Only true two-a-day and hard lower-body day. Threshold completion and recent lower lifting unverified: use conservative 3 × 6 min and restrained strength.

Duration
Run approximately 50–55 min; strength approximately 45–60 min, separated by at least 3 h
Volume
3 × 6 min threshold, 2 min jog recoveries; 11–12 strength working sets
Footwear / surface
Comfortable trainers on flat safe pavement/track for threshold; stable gym shoes for lift

Coach's note: No documented threshold splits or Tuesday lower-body strength last week. Use a consolidation dose, not 4 × 6 progression. First rep should feel controlled; a fourth rep should remain clearly possible. Lifting does not need to be a maximal-strength test.

AM — readiness and warm-up

  • Go/no-go: Green: normal gait, stable sleep/energy, hamstring, calf/Achilles, foot and adductor/hip pain 0–1/10 with no worsening. If questionable, choose amber. Do not treat unlogged prior work as completed.
  • Easy warm-up: 12–15 min jogging at RPE 2–3; ankle rockers 8/side; leg swings 8/side; walking lunges 5/side.
  • Warm-up strides: 2 × 20 seconds relaxed progressive strides around 5K rhythm, with ~60–90 sec easy jog. These are not maximal sprints.

AM — threshold

  • Main set: 3 × 6 min controlled threshold at RPE 6–7, with 2:00 very easy jogging between reps. Begin conservatively. No pace chasing.
  • Record quality: Log each rep's pace, average HR, RPE, and whether an additional rep felt possible. If rep 1 exceeds RPE 7.5, slow down; if form deteriorates, finish easy.
  • Cooldown: 10–15 min very easy jogging; no extra mileage.

PM — controlled primary strength

  • Back squat: After warm-up sets, 2 × 4 at RPE 7; 3 min rest; smooth reps, no grinder. Avoid if lower-body soreness or tissue symptoms.
  • Barbell bench press: 3 × 5 at RPE 7–7.5; 2½–3 min rest. Account for Monday incline pressing; reduce if shoulders/chest are fatigued.
  • Rear-foot-elevated split squat: 2 × 5 each leg at RPE 6.5–7; 90–120 sec rest. Omit if legs are significantly taxed by running.
  • Chest-supported row: 2 × 8–10 at RPE 7–8; 90 sec rest.
  • Standing calf raise — optional: 1–2 × 8–10 at RPE 7; 75–90 sec rest, only if calf/Achilles fully quiet. No AMRAP.

Fueling and readiness modifications

  • Between-session fueling: Eat carbohydrate plus 25–40 g protein after the run; a substantial meal and fluids/sodium commensurate with sweat loss during the separation. Target normal high-training-day intake.
  • Amber: 2 × 6 min threshold only if gait and tissues normal; otherwise 35–45 min easy. Limit lift to bench/rows; no squat, RFESS or calf loading when leg response is uncertain.
  • Red / stop: No hard running or heavy legs for sharp/worsening/gait-altering pain, illness, or severe fatigue; rest or pain-free very easy non-impact. No makeup intervals.

Medium-long easy + optional uphill strides

Aerobic durability first. Only with fully green lower legs, hamstring and adductor, finish with up to four 8-sec controlled uphill strides; walk off, no jogging cooldown.

Duration
65–70 min easy running; add only the time needed for 0–4 optional hill strides and walking recoveries
Volume
65–70 min RPE 2–3 easy + up to 4 × 8 sec uphill at 85–90% only fully green
Footwear / surface
Cushioned trainers on predictable flat/gentle route; safe dry ~3–5% hill for optional strides

Coach's note: This is an aerobic durability session, not a high-speed workout. A-week true acceleration/flying sprint remains the maximum-velocity exposure. Finish with walking after hills—no additional jogging cooldown.

Aerobic volume

  • Readiness check: Tuesday soreness is improving and gait normal. If not, shorten and omit all strides. No lifting Thursday.
  • Easy run: 65–70 min RPE 2–3, flat or gentle rolling terrain, first 10–15 min especially relaxed. No tempo, moderate progression or fast finish.
  • Pace cue: Conversation possible in full sentences; prioritize controlled duration over speed.

Optional strides at END — green tissues only

  • Gate: Only if calf/Achilles, hamstring, adductor/hip and foot remain 0–1/10 and mechanics are springy, no protective gait or unusual soreness.
  • Uphill strides: 0–4 × 8 sec controlled uphill accelerations on moderate ~3–5% grade; smoothly build to ~85–90% effort, not max; 2–3 min walking back/standing recovery between.
  • Finish: Walk a few minutes, done. No easy jog after the last hill. If legs are heavy after easy run, omit strides entirely.

Fueling and alternatives

  • Fuel: Normal carb-containing meal/snack beforehand as needed; hydration per conditions, protein + carbohydrate after. Midrun carbohydrate optional at this intensity/duration unless underfueled.
  • Amber: 45–55 min easy running with no hills; use non-impact cardio for impact soreness.
  • Red / stop: Stop the strides for calf/Achilles tightness, hamstring grabbing, asymmetry or altered push-off. Stop running for sharp or escalating pain.
