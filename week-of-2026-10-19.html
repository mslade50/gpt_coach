Oct 19–25 · B Week 2

Aerobic rebuild and 10K development. Tuesday is the only hard leg day and only two-a-day; Wednesday is low-impact recovery. Readiness modifications always take priority.

Monday — Upper A + easy run

Incline DB press 3×8–10, neutral pulldown 3×8–10, chest-supported row 2×10–12, lateral raise 2×15–20, curls 2×10–15, pressdowns 2×10–15. All RPE 7–8, 60–90 sec rests. Lift first, then 40–45 min easy running RPE 2–3.

Tuesday — Threshold + primary strength

Warm up 12–15 min easy with drills and 3×20 sec relaxed strides. 4×6 min threshold RPE 6–7, 2 min easy jog; cooldown 10–12 min. Record pace/HR/RPE and finish capable of another rep. Lift 3+ hours later: back squat 2×4–5 RPE 7–7.5; bench 4×5 RPE 7.5–8; RFESS 1–2×5/leg RPE 6.5–7; supported row 3×8–10; calf raise 2×8–10 only if Achilles/calf quiet. Compound rests 2.5–3 min; accessory rests 75–120 sec.

Wednesday — Recovery

50–60 min easy bike or elliptical, RPE 2–3. No lifting, intervals or running. Shorten to 30–45 min or rest if still unusually fatigued.

Thursday — Easy medium-long + hills

65–70 min conversational RPE 2–3. ONLY if legs, calf/Achilles and hamstrings are entirely green: 4×8 sec controlled uphill strides around 85–90% effort on a safe 3–5% grade, 2–3 min walking recovery. Walk after last hill; no jogging cooldown. Otherwise omit hills; if sore reduce to 45–55 min easy.

Friday — Upper B + aerobic

Lift first: shoulder press 3×8–10, pulldown 3×8–10, DB chest press 3×8–12, supported row 3×10–12 at RPE 7–8, 75–90 sec rests. Optional rear delts 2×15–20, curls 2×10–15 and pressdowns 2×10–15. Then 40–45 min easy run or 50–60 min easy bike/elliptical; do not do both.

Saturday — Long easy run

85 min easy RPE 2–3, first 15 min deliberately slow, no fast finish. 90 min is a ceiling only with excellent recovery. About 30–45 g carbohydrate/hour during, plus fluids/sodium as needed. Amber: 65–75 min.

Sunday — Off

Full rest. Record completed work, sleep, soreness and tissue response before the next A-week speed day.

Green = normal mechanics and quiet tissues; amber = reduce volume and omit hills/intensity; red = stop impact for sharp, escalating or gait-altering pain. No make-up mileage.
