Mon, October 26 — Upper A + easy run

One continuous training window; lift first, then easy running. Do not create Tuesday leg fatigue.

Duration: 90–105 min   ·   Footwear: Gym footwear, then trainers

Lift first and run second in the same visit. Easy effort is the priority; no strides, tempo or fast finish.

Readiness

  • Before training: Normal gait. If lower legs are painful or Tuesday freshness seems compromised, swap the run for easy cycling or shorten it.

Upper A — lift first

  • Incline dumbbell press: 3 × 8–10 @ RPE 7–8; 90 sec rests, leave 2 reps in reserve.
  • Neutral-grip pulldown / comfortable unweighted pull-up: 3 × 8–10 @ RPE 7–8; 90 sec rest. Respect shoulder range.
  • Chest-supported row: 2 × 10–12 @ RPE 7–8; 75–90 sec rests.
  • Lateral raise: 2 × 15–20; 45–60 sec rests.
  • Dumbbell/cable curl: 2 × 10–15; 60 sec rests, finish at 1–2 RIR.
  • Cable pressdown: 2 × 10–15; 60 sec rests, finish at 1–2 RIR.

Easy running

  • Run: 40–45 min RPE 2–3, conversational. First 10 min extra easy; flat/gently rolling route. No fast finish.

Fueling / contingency

  • Fuel: Carb-containing meal/snack before; ~25–40 g protein and normal carbohydrate after. Drink for conditions.
  • Amber: 2 sets per upper movement and 30–35 min easy running or cycling.
  • Red: Rest for illness, systemic fatigue, or worsening/gait-altering pain; upper-only may be appropriate for isolated pain-free lower-body limitation.

Tue, October 27 — Acceleration + flying sprints; primary strength

Only genuinely high leg day and only planned two-a-day. Flying reps require fully green tissues and recent sprint tolerance.

Duration: Track ~50–70 min; lift ~50–65 min, separated by 3+ hours   ·   Footwear: Trainers on dry predictable track; gym shoes later

Green-day full speed is conditional. Recent sprint completion is unverified, so if fast running has been absent, perform the conservative amber starts only. Full recovery between fast reps; never sprint while fatigued.

Readiness and warm-up

  • Go/no-go: Full sprint plan ONLY with normal gait, recovered legs, and hamstring, Achilles/calf, foot and adductor/hip pain 0–1/10; no stiff or guarded accelerations. Confirm recent sprint tolerance in your own log.
  • Easy jog + mobility: 12–15 min easy jogging; ankle rocker 8/side, leg swings 8/side, walking lunge 5/side.
  • Sprint drills: A-march 20 m, A-skip 20 m, low dribble 20 m. Green only: 2 × 6 low pogos.
  • Build-ups: 4 progressive build-ups 40–50 m (~60%, ~75%, ~85%, ~90%) with walk-back and full recovery.

True speed — GREEN only

  • Acceleration: 3 × 20–30 m two-point starts at ~93–97% of current maximum; full 4–5 min walk/standing recovery. No reaching or straining.
  • Flying 20s: 2 × 20 m fly with 30–35 m progressive build-up and 30–40 m gradual deceleration; ~95–97% current top-speed feel, with 5–7 min full rest. No timed testing required.
  • Exit criteria: Stop immediately for localized pain, protective mechanics, unusual loss of smoothness, or clear rep-to-rep slowdown. Walk several minutes afterward; no need to add mileage to the track session.

Strength — later, separate window

  • Deadlift: Warm up gradually, then 2 × 3 @ RPE 7–7.5; 3–4 min rest. No grinders or technical deterioration.
  • Barbell bench press: 4 × 5 @ RPE 7.5–8; 2.5–3 min rest.
  • Rear-foot elevated split squat: 2 × 5 per leg @ RPE 7; 90–120 sec rest. Keep load modest after sprinting.
  • Chest-supported row: 3 × 8–10 @ RPE 7–8; 90 sec rests.
  • Standing calf raise: 2 × 8–10 @ RPE 7; 75–90 sec rests, only if Achilles/calf are completely quiet.

Fueling / contingency

  • Between sessions: A carbohydrate-rich meal plus 25–40 g protein after track; an additional ~60–100 g carbohydrate over the gap is reasonable depending on normal total intake, appetite and time; replenish fluids/sodium for sweat.
  • Amber / unverified recent sprint exposure: 2 × 20 m controlled accelerations at ~85–92%, with 4–5 min rest; NO flying reps, pogo jumps or hard lower-body lift. Bench and supported row can remain if otherwise recovered.
  • Red: No fast running or heavy legs for pain, illness, gait change or systemic fatigue. Rest or non-impact easy activity if appropriate. No make-up sprint reps.

Thu, October 29 — Controlled subthreshold aerobic

Medium aerobic stimulus without threshold strain or lifting. On A weeks the speed work remains Tuesday.

Duration: 50–60 min   ·   Footwear: Comfortable trainers on flat or gently rolling, predictable terrain

Target sustainable controlled effort, not last month's pace. Finish able to complete another repetition or two. No hill sprints, no lifting.

Readiness

  • Tuesday response: Start the quality portion only when Tuesday soreness is clearly improving and stride is normal. If legs still heavy, run easy instead.

Warm-up and subthreshold

  • Warm-up: 12–15 min easy running (RPE 2–3).
  • Main set: 3 × 8 min at RPE 5–6 with 2 min easy jogging recoveries (between reps). Even breathing, relaxed form; pace is secondary.
  • Effort cap: If the first rep feels >RPE 6.5, ease back. No threshold or 5K effort. Finish with reserve.
  • Cooldown: 10–12 min easy jog, adjusting to keep total near 50–60 min. No extra mileage or fast finish.

Fueling / contingency

  • Fuel: Normal carbohydrate-containing meal/snack before if needed; rehydrate and eat protein/carbohydrate after.
  • Amber: 40–50 min easy running, RPE 2–3, no work reps.
  • Impact red: Rest or 40–50 min comfortable non-impact aerobic if pain-free. Stop for pain escalating or altering gait.
